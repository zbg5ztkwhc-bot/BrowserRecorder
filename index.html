Access Teams

Microsoft Teams

Open Teams web app or desktop app

Recording Workflow:
  1. Click "Teams Web App" to open Teams in new tab
  2. Sign in and join your meeting
  3. Return here and click "Start Recording"
  4. Select the Teams tab/window to record
  5. Choose output resolution (adjustable during recording)
  6. Stop and download your recording
Audio Note: System audio (meeting sound) is included when you select "Share system audio" during screen capture setup.
